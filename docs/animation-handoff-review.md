# Animation handoff review

The supplied archive matches `heaveball-animation-preview-handoff.zip` on GitHub byte for byte. It presents 49 clips across six groups. All 49 clip IDs are available in the separate 3D Matchroom library. The Matchroom build now publishes the original standalone reel at `heaveball-latest-animation-preview.html`.

## Which versions to use

| Reel group | Clips | Best fit in gameplay |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Failed tackles | 6 | The backward grab whiff, escape, lunge miss and broken hit give distinct reactions when the carrier is retreating. Keep the 3D run-through for a stronger carrier driving through contact and the 3D turf and get-up sequences for completed tackles. |
| Diving catches and tips | 13 | Use the matching forward, backward or lateral reach from the reel. Use the 3D grounding and body spacing to finish the motion in live play. Select a tip when possession stays loose and a catch when it is secured. |
| Gambles | 2 | Use the late interception for a clean gamble and the incomplete dive for a miss. A gamble that only gets a hand to the ball benefits from the loose-deflection sequence. |
| Dropped balls | 20 | Use the contested catch, contested deflection and failed-interception variants when the corresponding event occurs. They communicate who reached the ball and why it stayed loose. |
| Boundary saves | 4 | The one-handed, back-landing and basketball-style saves are useful reference choreography. Matchroom currently attempts an airborne **kick** save, so its foot-led clip fits both the successful kick and the missed attempt. |
| Ball behavior | 4 | Keep the live 3D physics for match bounces. It accounts for the surface at impact, diminishing rebounds and the transition into a roll. Use the four reel clips to inspect trajectories in isolation. |

Comparing the clips by ID across 23 sample times per clip showed pose changes in all 13 diving catch/tip clips, both gambles, all 20 dropped-ball clips and all four boundary saves. One of the six failed tackle clips showed a pose change. A sampled pose difference indicates a revision, while event fit and the landing sequence determine which version reads best in play.

## Coverage and gaps

- The reel's selected playlist leaves out parts of current gameplay that already have 3D treatment. These include short underhand tosses and catches, low and high short-hop pickups, handoffs, blocking exchanges and blocks in the back, contact-aware tackles, fumble scoop and run-out or dive and secure, ring reaches, and Center celebrations.
- The live 3D update now uses the direction and outcome for tips, errant passes, dropped interceptions, contested deflections and deflected gambles. It uses separate backward variants for a grab whiff, lunge miss, escape or broken big hit. Completed tackles and dives still use the live turf landing and get-up choreography.
- A failed strip, a gamble that briefly gains and loses control, and a manual hand-style boundary save have authored reference clips but no distinct match outcome. Exact variants for those cases require gameplay decisions before they can be assigned. Matchroom already distinguishes a clean gamble interception, a missed gamble, and a gamble that deflects the ball.
- Replayable clips for the full set of 3D additions are a remaining library gap. The live choreography for paired tackle landings, the run-through and the two fumble recoveries is driven by match events.
